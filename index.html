Committees & Agendas

LOK SABHA

Agenda: Deliberation on the Future of Reservation Policy in India: Balancing Social Justice, Meritocracy, and Economic Equity

Overview: This committee will review India's reservation policies, assessing their impact on social justice and merit, and discuss possible reforms for the future.

SOCIAL REFORM COUNCIL

Agenda: Questioning Orthodox Practices in India: balancing Tradition, Rationality, and Social Progress

Overview: This council will assess orthodox practices in India, debating whether they preserve culture or hinder social progress, and suggest reforms for a more inclusive and progressive society.

WORLD HEALTH ORGANIZATION

Agenda: Mind Matters: Addressing the Mental Health Crisis Among Gen Z in the Digital Age.

Overview: This council will examine Gen Z's mental health challenges, focusing on digital media, social pressures, and societal expectations, while discussing strategies to promote awareness, resilience, and better support systems.

ALL INDIA STAKEHOLDER'S MEET

Agenda: Nepotism or Networking? Privilege, Access, and Fairness in India's Influencer and Entertainment Economy

Overview: This session will examine how privilege and networks shape careers in India's entertainment and digital industries, questioning whether talent alone ensures success or if nepotism still dominates opportunities.

*PITCH TANK*

Agenda: Deliberation & Investment in innovative entrepreneurial ventures through competitive pitching and valuation.

Overview: Pitch Tank is a dynamic online platform where young innovators pitch startups, social initiatives, or creative ideas to real investors and mentors. Participants face live feedback and tough questions while competing for funding, mentorship, and collaboration—turning bold ideas into real impact.

About & Founder's vision

Rishita Singh

Founder

Reform was born from a simple belief that questioning is not rebellion, and change does not begin with noise, but with clarity. It was because i wanted to challenge the way we accept things just because they've always been that way. Reform, to me, means looking at the systems around us and asking: "Does this really work? Does this serve everyone fairly? Can we make it better?"

I believe progress comes when we question, when we act, and when we take responsibility. It's about correcting what's broken, improving what's outdated, and building something that actually benefits society. Everything we do at reform every discussion, every program, every initiative is meant to turn ideas into impact. Even the resources we generate are meant to serve society, to fund projects, opportunities, and programs that help people, not just institutions.

To me, Reform isn't a one-time conference. It's mindset, a way of approaching life, society, and responsibility. And this is just the beginning.

QUOTE 2026 – "To create a platform where young minds come together to question, deliberate, and transform society. We envision a conference that inspires critical thinking, challenges outdated norms, and empowers the next generation to lead with courage, creativity, and a commitment to social progress."
